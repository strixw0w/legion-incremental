# S2 — proposed rule resolutions

**Version 1.0, 2026-11-13. Status: proposed candidate rules (historical).** The owner's decisions are recorded in `S2_OWNER_RESOLUTIONS.md` v1.0 (S2.1); that document governs.

Each rule is needed to simulate S2, and the approved documents leave its exact form open. Each states its consequence if changed ("do not silently invent gameplay rules"). All numbers are candidates in `content/w1-slice-s2.candidate.v0.2.0.json`. No candidate coefficient is approved.

| ID | Topic | Proposed rule (candidate) | Basis | Consequence if changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2-R01 | Character identity | `hero`; recruits `rec:<n>` in acquisition order, never reused; rare heroes `rare:<key>`. Random streams are keyed by these identities, never by content IDs. | R01.07, R03.16 | Identities are part of save data and of every event; renaming them would break replay compatibility. |
| S2-R02 | First recruit | Quest Q1: defeat 40 enemies in zone 1 (squad or worker kills). It grants `rec:1` free, productive slot 1 and deploy cap 1. | R03.03 (2–5 min target), R07.01 | A smaller N gives an earlier first recruit (S2-E08 sweeps 25 / 40 / 60). |
| S2-R03 | Productive slots | Slot 2 at zone 2 access, slot 3 at zone 3 access. Slots 4–6 belong to later W1 zones (not in S2 content). | R03.04–05, R10.04 | Earlier slots mean earlier legion scaling. |
| S2-R04 | Recruit purchase | Gold: 150 for the 2nd recruit, 400 for the 3rd. One idempotent ledger transaction (`recruit:<n>`). A new recruit is level 1 and classless. | R03.04 | Cost controls when squads grow (S2-E05 sensitivity). |
| S2-R05 | Deploy cap | 1 after Q1, 2 at zone 2 access, 3 at zone 3 access. The hero is not counted. | R10.05, R02.04 | A separate gate from slots, as approved; it decides hero-plus-recruits timing. |
| S2-R06 | Hero class choice | Available from hero level 5; the first choice is free. | R03.02 | An earlier choice gives earlier class strength. |
| S2-R07 | Class change | The first assignment is free; each later change costs 100 gold. Level, EXP and HP fraction are preserved; mastery and talents are kept per class; refused in combat. | R03.09, D-05 | The cost decides how often policies switch (S2-E04). |
| S2-R08 | Mastery | Mastery EXP for the held class equals the combat EXP earned while holding it. The mastery level uses the combat EXP table. | D-05, R05.16 | A separate mastery curve would change record pace, not abuse safety. |
| S2-R09 | Class records | Per character: mastery level per class (monotone). Per account: the maximum over all owned characters, reserve included. Tracked only; no bonus applied in S2. | R03.17, R05.16, R-A5, owner §8 | Applying bonuses is S4. |
| S2-R10 | Talents | 1 point per level above 1, allocated per class. Two talents per class: Warrior Vitality (+5% HP) and Might (+3% attack); Archer Precision (+1% crit) and Might; Mage Arcana (+5% ability damage) and Might. At most 10 points each. Respec is free, with a 60 s cooldown, for the current class only. | R03.10–11 | The talent tables are placeholders; only the mechanics are under test. |
| S2-R11 | Class modifiers | Warrior: HP +40%, defence +2 + 50% per level, attack −10%, interval 1,300 ms. Archer: crit +10 points, HP −10%, interval 1,000 ms. Mage: attack +25%, HP −20%, interval 1,500 ms. | R03.01 roles | Class strength sensitivity (S2-E02). |
| S2-R12 | Abilities | One per class: Cleave 200% / 8 s; Power Shot 250% / 6 s; Fireball 400% (+5% per Arcana point) / 10 s. Recruits cast automatically; the hero casts only by policy command. One hit draw and one crit draw per cast. | R01.03, R02.10–11 | Ability strength moves damage share (S2-E02). |
| S2-R13 | Enemy threat order | The enemy attacks living Warriors first (in acquisition order), then the hero, then the others (in acquisition order). | Bible §4 (aggro open) | Without a tank rule, the hero absorbs all damage. |
| S2-R14 | Knockout and recovery | A recruit at 0 HP is knocked out: no attacks, no targeting, no later credit. It recovers to full HP 20 s after the knockout, or at the end of the encounter in progress then, whichever is later; immediately at the hero's respawn. No loss. | D-03 option A | Longer recovery gives smaller effective squads (S2-E07). |
| S2-R15 | EXP eligibility | Eligible: deployed and present at the encounter's start, and not knocked out before the killing batch. Knocked out in the killing batch counts as eligible (simultaneous blows). Each eligible character gets the full EXP. | D-04, R03.12, owner §7 | A per-damage contribution rule would be party play (M9). |
| S2-R16 | Deployment changes | Leaving the squad is immediate (the member's pending blows go stale). Joining counts from the next engagement. | R07.10 | Mid-fight joining would need partial-eligibility rules. |
| S2-R17 | Workers | A numerical one-on-one stream per worker in an accessible zone. Same combat rules; engage overhead between fights; KO recovery 30 s; 3 knockouts in 5 min stop the worker with a reason. Own encounter IDs and random streams. Kills count for zone progress and quests. | R-A4, D-04, R06.07 | Recovery and stop rules decide worker safety (S2-E05, S2-E07). |
| S2-R18 | Commands during pending work | Assignment changes are accepted at any time; the character's old scheduled work goes stale and no credit is created by the switch. Class, talent and respec commands are refused while the character is in an encounter (`CHARACTER_BUSY`). | Owner §7–8 | Allowing loadout changes mid-fight would need mid-fight stat recomputation rules. |
| S2-R19 | Hero ability use by policies | Realistic "attentive" policies cast the hero's ability whenever it is ready in combat. "Passive" policies never do. | R01.03 | It bounds how much manual play adds. |
| S2-R20 | Rare heroes | Unique per account. A duplicate grant gives `res.rare.hero_shard` × 1. They share productive slots; any extra stays in reserve. No content in S2; a diagnostic grant exists for tests only. | D-06, R03.14–16, owner §6 | — |
