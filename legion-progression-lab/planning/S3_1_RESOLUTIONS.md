# S3.1 — proposed rules and definitions

**Version 1.0, 2026-11-14. Status: proposed, awaiting the owner.** Rules needed by the S3.1 corrections and measurements (owner "Independent S3 Review and S3.1 Authorisation" §2–§10). Every number is a candidate.

| ID | Topic | Proposed rule | Basis | Consequence if changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S3.1-R01 | Crafting eligibility | Instant crafting is an **account menu action by a named crafter who must be free**. It is **refused, never interrupting anything**, when the crafter is in the reserve (`CRAFTER_IN_RESERVE`), knocked out (`CRAFTER_KNOCKED_OUT`), in an encounter (`CHARACTER_BUSY`), or has another productive activity (`CRAFTER_WORKING`): a gathering action or production unit in progress, a gathering or independent-combat assignment, or the hero gathering. An assigned smith with no unit in progress, an idle recruit, a squad member between fights and the hero between fights may craft. Reserve characters never gain profession EXP. Managing a reserve character's equipment stays allowed. | owner S3.1 §2 (asks for an explicit choice); R16 | The alternative, "a craft interrupts the crafter's current work", would let a player trade an action in progress for an instant craft; "crafting is exempt" would let one character produce in two roles. Either is a small code change. |
| S3.1-R02 | Recipe validation | No resource twice in one list; summed recoverable ≤ summed input; a timed recipe returns strictly less of any input it also outputs; intermediates checked (spec v1.1 §7) | owner S3.1 §3 | Allowing duplicate lines would need normalisation before every check |
| S3.1-R03 | Effective damage | Fractional proportional attribution (spec v1.1 §5a); the S3 integer method is kept as "legacy" | owner S3.1 §4 | — |
| S3.1-R04 | First simple crafted item | The first Crafted entry of any item (tools included) | owner §8 metric 1 | — |
| S3.1-R05 | First crafted equipment equipped | The first Equipped or AutoEquipped entry placing a crafted (non-diagnostic) item on a character | owner §8 metric 2 | — |
| S3.1-R06 | **Meaningful combat equipment upgrade** (defined before any S3.1 run) | An equip of a weapon, armour piece or accessory that raises the wearer's **matched-zone combat value by at least 10%**, where combat value = expected damage per second against the enemy of the hero's current zone (hit chance × damage per hit × crits ÷ attack interval, from the wearer's own stats) **or** expected hits survived against that enemy (max HP ÷ that enemy's damage per hit after defence), comparing the wearer's stats with the slot's previous item against its stats with the new item. Tools never count. | owner §8 metric 3 ("declared stat improvement or matched-state combat improvement") | A higher threshold delays the milestone; a squad-level measure would discount upgrades to minor squad members |
| S3.1-R07 | First Copper combat item equipped | The first equip of a Copper weapon, armour piece or accessory | owner §8 metric 4 | — |
| S3.1-R08 | Capacity and utilisation | Maximum gathering capacity = units per hour of a character gathering continuously at its node with its current efficiency; maximum smithing capacity = units per hour of a smith working continuously. Utilisation = time with work in progress ÷ time assigned to the role, connected time only. | owner §7 | — |
| S3.1-R09 | Excess gathering | Units gathered beyond the declared planned demand (the policy's outstanding plan when it chose the role; for fixed-role policies, the total later consumed) | owner §7 | — |
| S3.1-R10 | Shortage types | Input shortage = a job paused for its inputs; output-demand shortage = a smith assigned with no job to work (no demand) | owner §7 | — |
| S3.1-R11 | Combat-effective equipment value | For each character, the percentage gain in matched-zone damage per second from its equipped items over the same character without them; summed over the deployed squad and the hero | owner §10 | — |
| S3.1-R12 | Investment payback milestone | The first active minute at which a strategy's cumulative legion combat EXP reaches POL3-CONQ's at the same minute on the same seeds (matched-state diagnostic with industrial recruits returned to combat after the investment) | owner §10 | A hero-EXP milestone gives a different crossing time; both are reported |
