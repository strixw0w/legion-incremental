## 6. Owner §14 balancing questions
1. **Do Monuments make pure combat disproportionately stronger?**
   - **Not in the fresh account:** POL4-MONUMENT is −0.2% against POL3-CONQ, because kills are spawn-limited.
   - **In the damage-limited arena:** +9.1% legion EXP for 306 gold.
   - Monument spending is small because the material milestones block it (§5.3). **Whether Monuments would be disproportionate at intended spending is not yet answerable:** the gold costs are too cheap against income, and the milestones decide everything.
2. **Do industrial material milestones create useful demand for earlier resources?**
   - **Partly.** POL4-MONUMENT used 22.3 slime gel and 10 wolf fang, from combat drops it already held.
   - **But in these policies, demand becomes a stall rather than activity.** No policy goes back to farm fangs, and the combat policies never make planks or bars, so milestone-blocked time is about 160 of 180 min (3 days) and about 400 of 420 min (7 days).
   - A real player may go and gather. These policies do not.
3. **Do Bestiary bonuses make earlier-zone farming attractive?**
   - **The bonuses are reachable:** POL4-BESTIARY farmed slime and wolf to 600 / 600 kills (against 100 / 120), with 9 Bestiary rewards against 5 and attack pool 2,900 bp against 2,050.
   - **But it costs −8.3% legion EXP over 3 days** in the spawn-limited account.
   - **Payback beyond 3 days was not measured** (deferred to S6, owner §4D).
4. **Do passive Feat rewards overwhelm purchased upgrades?** Share of the permanent bonus at 7 days:
   - **Version 1 POL4-BALANCED:** Feats 26.7%, class records 32.4% and roster 3.4%, against Monuments 20.4%. Passive milestones lead, because Monument purchases stalled.
   - **Version 1.1:** Monuments lead (39.6–46.7%).
   - **No system exceeds 50%** except arena POL4-BALANCED2: Monuments 50.2% (an H-S4-06 red flag, §7).
5. **Do highest-class-record bonuses encourage a diverse legion?**
   - **Modestly.** A mixed squad earns 9 of 9 class rewards; an all-Warrior squad earns 6.
   - Not diversifying costs 500 bp of ability bonus and 1.5% legion EXP over 3 days.
6. **Does stronger equipment become obsolete too quickly as combat levels rise?** **Not measured in S4:** no obsolescence measure was registered. POL3-DEMAND2 makes 18.5 meaningful upgrades and equips 25 items in 3 days. That shows frequent replacement but does not answer the question. **Left open.**
7. **Do the new systems worsen the already-fast early progression?** **Slightly faster, by a small amount.** Permanent on against off:

   | Measure | POL3-CONQ | POL3-DEMAND2 |
   |---|---|---|
   | Level at 15 min | 17.0 (off 16.2) | 12.8 (off 12.0) |
   | Zone unlocks and minutes to level 10 | unchanged | unchanged |
   | Day-1 legion EXP | +1.5% | +0.8% |
   | 3-day total | +0.5% | +0.2% |
8. **Do different investments remain viable under matched constraints?**
   - **Fresh account:** pure combat and Monument investment are within 0.3%. Demand-aware industry and balanced progression cost about 4%, and Bestiary farming 8%. Over-gathering equipment (POL3-EQUIP) costs 59%.
   - **Arena:** Monuments +9%. The industrial policies lose 58–62%, through a policy deadlock (§9.1).
   - The industry result does not show that industry is non-viable. It shows that this policy stalls when its inputs come too slowly.
   - **Best feasible strategy found** (not a global optimum): POL3-CONQ in the fresh account, and POL4-MONUMENT2 in the arena (+9.2%).

## 9. Missed targets and limitations
1. **The DEMAND2 arena deadlock.** In the arena, POL3-DEMAND2 and POL4-BALANCED(2) request equipment whose inputs arrive too slowly. The smith waits for inputs for the rest of the run (about 3 of 43.5 requested units never complete). This explains:
   - the −58% to −62% arena industry results;
   - the arena elasticity warnings.

   It is a policy-and-content interaction, reported and not fixed. Fixing it would be a policy change for a later, registered increment.
2. **Material milestones freeze Monument progress after day 1** under every policy (§5.3, §5.4). Gold costs are trivial by comparison (the full Power track is 18,440 gold, about 79 active min of income). This is a candidate-content finding for the owner. It was not tuned.
3. **H-S4-07 missed and H-S4-08 partial** (§5.5). No delays were added.
4. **H-S4-06 red flags** (§7): the interval artefact and the 50.2% share.
5. **Post-results policy version 1.1** (amendment 01). It is a follow-up registered after the results. The version 1 results stand as registered.
