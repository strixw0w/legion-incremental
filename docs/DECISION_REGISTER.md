## Reconciliation notes
- R10.05 specifies the launch deployment cap of three; R02.04 remains the later six-deployed target. Hero is additional.
- R10.04 sets the World 1 roster target at six; R10.07 requires three discoverable named heroes. Whether rare heroes use the six slots and how swapping works remains O01, not an additional selection.
- R10.06 defers the R03.07–08 branch advancement content; no advanced classes at launch.
- R10.09 defers Nightmare, R06.18 remains the long-term difficulty structure.
- R07.04 approves two offline windows, not a specific reduced-window rate. 25% is only a candidate.
- R05.16 supersedes an interpretation based on merely switching an already high-level character to every class.
- R10.03 prototype miniboss does not increase the R06.14 launch major boss count.
- Exact costs, stats, talents, named heroes, enemy names other than W1 theme, numeric timings outside selected targets and monetisation prices remain provisional.
